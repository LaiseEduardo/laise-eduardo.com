## Scope

Surface: the single page at `/` (site/index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers for QA / test-automation roles scanning dozens of CVs on a laptop in daylight. Job: decide in 60 seconds whether to contact Laise. Action: email / GitHub / LinkedIn. Proof: polar-bear, eleven11 (live, 88%+ coverage), 420finder, Zelo; AWS/Terraform/CI in every repo. Constraints: English, static HTML/CSS/JS, WCAG AA, light default, dark via system, 360px up, no photo, no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Laise is presented as a specified, checked engineering part on a drawing sheet: every claim has a tolerance and a "checked by" link. It refuses the default developer portfolio (hero + skills grid + three rounded cards).

OWN-WORLD: Vellum white sheet (#fbfbf9) inside a hairline ink frame with zone letters A–D / 1–6 on the border; drafting ink near-black (#0d1b2a); one accent, drafting blue (#1565c0) for links, callout leaders, and the primary action; cool grey (#9aa4ae) for hairlines and dimension text. Display: Big Shoulders Display (condensed drafting capitals, uppercase, tracked). Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible. Measurements and revision data: JetBrains Mono, only for numbers, dates, coverage. Components: title block (bordered table of cells), callout leaders (thin line + dot pointing at an item), revision table (experience), dimension lines (skill groups), tolerance notes. States invert to solid ink. Dark mode: blueprint inversion (ink on #0f1a2b with white hairlines), same accent lifted.

STORY: Visitor lands, reads the title block: who, what, where, checked by GitHub. Believes she is precise and ships real systems because the sheet documents proof with links. Acts: email or GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width sheet with frame and zone letters. Top-left of the sheet: a large callout-style heading "LAISE EDUARDO" in drafting capitals with the title beneath as a dimension string. Bottom-right (desktop) / below (mobile): the title block, 2×3 cells: NAME, TITLE, LOCATION, CHECKED BY (GitHub, LinkedIn, Email links), REV (date), SHEET 1/1. Primary action "Email Laise" sits in the title block's last cell as a solid-ink button. Three proof lines with callout leaders pointing at the title block: "88% coverage, eleven11", "Playwright framework, polar-bear", "AWS + Terraform, 4 repos".

FORM: Engineering drawing title block, candidate 1 of my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned candidate 7, QC inspection tag). Seed key 637171f8. Raises kept from the hand: billing-order hierarchy for experience and projects (zoot handbill); every claim seamed to its evidence link (quilt); one-bit inverted states (HyperCard).

Signature interaction: hovering a project or skill draws its callout leader to the title block; focus does the same.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
